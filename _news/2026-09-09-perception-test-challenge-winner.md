Congratulations! Team **Banana Bats** — Minseon Park, Jian Kim, Inhong Paek, Changwoo Jeon, Jaeyun Shin and Jong Chul Ye — won the **Grounded Video-QA track** of the [Fourth Perception Test Challenge](https://perception-test-challenge.github.io/), organised by Google DeepMind and held at ECCV 2026 in Malmö, Sweden on 9 September 2026. The challenge drew 1,439 submissions from 125 teams across its four tracks.

What makes this result worth singling out is who produced it. **Minseon Park, Jian Kim, Inhong Paek and Changwoo Jeon are undergraduates who spent the summer at BISPL on the KAIST AI Internship (KAIRI) programme, and this first-place finish came out of that single vacation's work** --- undergraduate interns taking on an open international benchmark and coming first in their track, alongside BISPL graduate student Jaeyun Shin.

The team's [technical report](https://storage.googleapis.com/dm-perception-test/eccv_2026_technical_reports/The_Fourth_Perception_Test_challenge_ECCV2026_BananaBats%20-%20CHANGWOO%20JEON.pdf) is available from the challenge site.
