Congratulations! Prof. Jong Chul Ye has been recognized among the **World's Top 2% Scientists** in both the single-year and career-long categories of the 2026 Stanford--Elsevier citation rankings.

In the **single-year rankings (2025 citation impact)**, Prof. Ye ranked **4th worldwide and 1st in South Korea in Nuclear Medicine & Medical Imaging**. He also ranked **1st in South Korea in the broader field of Information & Communication Technologies (ICT)**, with Artificial Intelligence & Image Processing identified as his secondary research subfield. Across all scientific disciplines, he ranked **4,129th worldwide, 19th in South Korea, and 2nd at KAIST**.

These rankings, based on standardized citation indicators excluding self-citations, highlight the growing international impact of his research in medical imaging and artificial intelligence.

The underlying tables are available in the [Elsevier Data Repository](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/9) --- J. P. A. Ioannidis, *Updated Science-Wide Author Databases of Standardized Citation Indicators*, version 9 (October 2026).
