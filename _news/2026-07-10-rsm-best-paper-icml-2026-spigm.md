Congratulations! [*Reward Score Matching: Unifying Reward-based Fine-tuning for Flow and Diffusion Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17415), by Jeongjae Lee, Jinho Chang, Jeongsol Kim and Jong Chul Ye, received the **Best Paper Award** at the ICML 2026 Workshop on Structured Probabilistic Inference and Generative Modeling (SPIGM), held in Seoul in July 2026.
