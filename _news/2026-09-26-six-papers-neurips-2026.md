Congratulations! Six papers from BISPL have been accepted to the Fortieth Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2026).

- **Accelerating Video Inverse Problem Solvers with Autoregressive Diffusion Models** --- Taesung Kwon, Jonghyun Park, Hyungjin Chung, Jong Chul Ye
- **Aligning Few-Step Generative Model via Amortizing Sample-Based Variational Inference** --- Jaewoo Lee, Hyeongyu Kang, Dohyun Kim, Kyuil Sim, Woocheol Shin, Minsu Kim, Taeyoung Yun, Jeongjae Lee, Sanghyeok Choi, Tabitha Edith Lee, Jong Chul Ye, Jinkyoo Park
- **CRePE: Curved Ray Expectation Positional Encoding for Unified-Camera-Controlled Video Generation** --- Seonghyun Jin, Youngmin Kim, Sunwoo Park, Jong Chul Ye
- **MotionCFG: Boosting Motion Dynamics via Semantic Motion Sharpening** --- Byungjun Kim, Soobin Um, Jong Chul Ye
- **Accelerated Image Editing via Consistency-Aware Source Token Pruning** --- Jeongsol Kim, Jong Chul Ye, Yufei Wang, Jian Wang
- **[Reward Score Matching: Unifying Reward-based Fine-tuning for Flow and Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17415)** --- Jeongjae Lee, Jinho Chang, Jeongsol Kim, Jong Chul Ye

The last of these also took the [Best Paper Award at the ICML 2026 SPIGM workshop](/news/2026-07-10-rsm-best-paper-icml-2026-spigm/) in July.
